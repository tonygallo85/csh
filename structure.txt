COOL SCHOOL HUB - PAGES


PUBLIC  (anyone, no login)
├── Home - the course offer  (language, level, schedule)
├── Log in
└── Register


LOGGED IN  (student, teacher, admin)
├── Dashboard - "My courses"
├── Courses - the catalog
│   ├── Course details
│   ├── Add a course         (teacher, admin)
│   └── Edit a course        (teacher: own only, admin)
└── My profile


ADMIN ONLY
└── Users
    └── Edit a user          (name, email, promote to teacher)



WHO DOES WHAT

Student    sees their courses, enrolls in new ones, unenrolls
Teacher    sees the courses they teach, adds, edits and deletes them
Admin      sees and edits everything, promotes users to teacher
